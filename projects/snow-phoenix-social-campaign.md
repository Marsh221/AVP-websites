## Creative direction

A legendary Snow Phoenix embodies rebirth, stillness, ancient protection and celestial ascension. Keep the artwork premium and cinematic rather than cluttered. The phoenix is the hero; avoid excessive text, social handles, badges or decorative overlays.

**Palette:** ice white, silver, glacier blue, deep midnight blue.  
**Atmosphere:** moonlit glaciers, snow, mist, celestial light, ancient mountain temples.  
**Brand treatment:** subtle AVP signature only when a branded poster is specifically needed. For standalone artwork, no text, watermark or logo.
