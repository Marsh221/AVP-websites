# Crimson Dragon Awakening — Motion Study

**Studio:** Art Visionary Pixel (AVP)  
**Format:** Vertical cinematic fantasy short  
**Export:** 1080p MP4, 24 fps  
**Current cut:** Approximately 9.5 seconds  
**Genre:** Donghua-inspired dark fantasy / cultivation fantasy

## Project overview

A short cinematic experiment exploring the scale and presence of a colossal crimson Eastern dragon above an ancient mountain temple city. The visual direction combines crimson demonic energy, cold moonlight, drifting mist, embers, and the silhouette language of Chinese cultivation fantasy.

## Creative direction

- **Palette:** Crimson, black, molten gold, and cold moon-blue.
- **Atmosphere:** Mythic, ominous, cinematic, and monumental.
- **Motion:** Slow reveal, controlled camera movement, and a restrained ending.
- **AVP focus:** Treat AI-generated footage as raw material for visual storytelling, then refine pacing, continuity, color, and sound in the edit.

## Current edit notes

The current social cut is a short-form motion study. The dragon's eye direction is not yet individually corrected; the edit uses a tighter ending to avoid emphasizing the gaze shift. This is a practical interim edit, not a frame-by-frame eye replacement.
