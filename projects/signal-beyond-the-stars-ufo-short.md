## Project overview

A mysterious craft emerges from the darkness and illuminates an unknown world. The short is designed as a compact cinematic mystery, inviting viewers to consider whether the arrival is a visitor, a warning, or something beyond human understanding.

## Creative direction

- **Mood:** Mysterious, atmospheric, otherworldly and cinematic.
- **Subject:** An unidentified craft appearing from the darkness.
- **Story hook:** A signal from beyond the stars.
- **Editing:** Short-form vertical pacing with the source video's existing audio retained.
- **AVP approach:** Use AI-assisted visuals as material for visual storytelling, then shape the final experience through editing, pacing and sound.
